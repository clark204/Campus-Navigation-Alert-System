# Portal: settings, account menu, notifications, saved places, concerns

Read this before touching the student/faculty portal's Settings, the top bar account menu or notification bell, saved or recent places, or concerns (Help & contact and Admin > Concerns).

## Decisions
- The portal home has no room search (removed 2026-11-18 at the user's request; `place-search.js` is gone). Rooms are found on the campus map (/navigate).
- The portal home shows only real data (placeholders removed 2026-11-18): the next class for students and faculty (schedule.md), nothing about teaching for staff, "You can send emergency alerts" for trusted faculty and staff, the map's office rooms (no opening hours: the map has none), the map's IT rooms under "My college" for IT college users (`AppServiceProvider::IT_COLLEGE`), and the emergency numbers. There are no announcements until an announcements feature exists.
- The emergency alerts card shows on the portal home only while alerts are off on this device (`hideStates` on `partials/push-alerts`). Turning them off, or reading about a block, is in Settings → Alerts.
- **Account menu** (`partials/account-menu`, in the shared top bar): a `<details>` so it opens without JavaScript; `app.js` closes it on Escape, an outside click, or a link. Students and faculty get Profile, Email & password, Emergency alerts (the three Settings tabs), Saved places, Help & contact, Sign out. The admin gets My account, Settings, Sign out.
- **Portal Settings** (`/student/settings`, `/faculty/settings`, `Portal\SettingsController`): the admin settings design (same tabs, `settings-tabs.js`, `?tab=`). Tabs: Profile (read-only, from the COR; "Tell the administrator" opens Help & contact with the Account topic picked), Account (`my-account`: email and password), Alerts (on/off for this device and what each level does, from `AlertSeverity`), Navigation ("Voice directions" for this browser, localStorage, see navigation.md).
- Email and password changes go through one shared `AccountController` (with `Requests\Account\UpdateEmailRequest`, `UpdatePasswordRequest`) for every role; the rules live in `App\Services\AccountChanges`, shared with the app's `Api\AccountController` (registration.md). It redirects to `User::settingsUrl('my-account')`: the admin's or the user's portal settings. Same rules as the admin's (see settings.md). A new email is only saved after the code sent to it is entered (`partials/account-email-form`, registration.md "Email verification"). Portal pages wait until the user's email is verified (`EnsureEmailIsVerified`).
- Portal routes for both portals are registered in one loop in `routes/web.php` (`student.*` for students, `faculty.*` for faculty and staff). `UserRole::portal()` maps staff to the faculty portal.
- **Toasts** (`partials/toasts` in the portal, admin and index layouts, `resources/js/toasts.js`, `.toast` in app.css): every flash message is a toast, not a box on the page. `status` and `toast` (a string or `['title' => …, 'body' => …]`) are green success toasts that close by themselves (6 s, longer for long text, up to 12 s; the gold timer bar pauses on hover and focus). `warning` is a toast that stays until closed. Emergency alerts are NOT toasts (their own banner and takeover), and form errors stay next to their fields (including `admin/settings-message`, now errors only). Native `popover="manual"` (no library), bottom right (full width on phones), several stack upwards, announced once with `ariaNotify()` or a live region. Never shown over the alarm takeover, and closes if one opens. Tests: `ToastsTest`. Gotcha: a test that flashes as one user and then loads a page as another must `flushSession()`, or the toast shows up there.
- **Saved places**: `saved_places` (user, room, unique). Saved with the "Save" button next to "Where to?" on /navigate (signed in, destination is a room). `navigation/saved-places.js` changes the button at once and undoes it if the server refuses. Removed on the Saved places page.
- **Recent places**: `recent_places` (user, room, `used_at`, unique). Added when the user taps Start on /navigate (best effort, never blocks navigating). Only the newest 8 are kept (`RecentPlaces::KEEP`) so it never becomes a location history; the user can clear them.
- The portal home shows the newest 5 of each (`AppServiceProvider::HOME_PLACES`, a view composer). Rows show the room and `Room::locationLabel()` ("NEE Building · 2nd floor").
- **Help & contact is its own page** (`/student/help`, `/faculty/help`, `Portal\HelpController`, `portal/help-page`), not a footer on every portal page (changed 2026-11-18 at the user's request; `partials/help-footer` is gone). The sidebar and the account menu link to it; `?topic=` preselects a topic. It has the concern form, "Your messages" (the user's newest 5 concerns with Open or Resolved, and the administrator's replies under each; each has id `concern-{id}`), the administrator's contact details and the emergency numbers. Opening the page marks the user's reply notifications read.
- **Concerns**: `concerns` (user nullable, email, topic, message, resolved_at). Sent from the Help & contact page (topic chips + message; the reply goes to the account email) and from the landing page footer (guests type their email). Topics: `ConcernTopic`. 5 per 10 minutes per IP. Errors use the `concern` bag; success flashes `concern_status` and returns to the Help page, or to `#feedback` on the landing page.
- **Admin > Concerns is an email-style inbox** (Gmail layout, Clark's request 2026-11-21; `Admin\ConcernController`). The list (`admin/concerns-page`): folders Open and Resolved (`?status=resolved`) on the left (tabs on phones), 20 rows per page with "1–20 of N" and newer/older arrows. A row: avatar initials (`admin/concern-avatar`), sender (or "Visitor") with the message count when there are replies, topic chip, topic label – the start of the message, a replied arrow, and the time (today), date (this year) or n/j/y (older). Unread rows are bold with a maroon edge: unread = this admin still has an unread `ConcernReceived` bell notification for it (no extra column). The conversation (`/admin/concerns/{concern}`, `admin.concerns.show`, `admin/concern-page`): Back to the folder, Reply and Mark resolved / Reopen; the topic as the subject with its status; the message (sender, email, role and ID with "Open account"), the replies in order ("You to …"), and the reply box at the bottom ("To …", Send, "Mark as resolved" ticked by default; errors in the bag `reply{id}`). Opening it marks its bell notification read for this admin; replying or resolving marks it read for every admin. Replying returns to the conversation. The sidebar shows the open count.
- **Replies** (`concern_replies`: concern, admin `user_id` nullable, message; `ConcernReply`): each reply is emailed to the concern's email (`App\Mail\ConcernReplyMail`, queued, `mail/concern-reply` and `-text`: the reply, "You wrote" and, for account holders, "Open Help & contact") and, when the sender has an account, sent to their bell (`ConcernReplied`). Visitors get the email only. The mail needs the queue worker (registration.md, "Email verification").
- **Notification bell** (`partials/notification-bell` in the shared top bar, view composer in `AppServiceProvider`, `NotificationController`): Laravel database notifications (`notifications` table). Every notification's data has `title`, `body` and `url` (a path, not a full URL, so it survives an APP_URL change). Students and faculty get "The administrator replied" (`ConcernReplied`, opens Help & contact at the concern). Admins get "New message from …" for each new concern (`ConcernReceived`, sent to every admin, opens the conversation; read for that admin once opened, for all admins once one replies or resolves it). Shows the unread count (gold badge: red is for emergencies only) and the newest 8. Opening one (`GET /notifications/{id}`) marks it read and follows its url (own notifications only, 404 otherwise; only paths on this site). "Mark all as read" is `POST /notifications/read`. A `<details>` like the account menu (`app.js` closes both on Escape, outside click or a link). Kept 90 days (scheduled in `routes/console.php`); deleted with the account (`AccountDeletion`).
- A concern stays when its sender's account is deleted (`user_id` set to null).
- **Shared with the app**: the inbox rules are in `App\Services\ConcernInbox` (`folder`, `unreadIds`, `markOpened`, `reply`, `resolve`, `reopen`) and `Requests\Admin\ReplyConcernRequest` (bag `reply{id}` on the web). App API (admin only): `GET /api/admin/concerns?status=resolved&page=` (rows with `unread`; meta has the open, resolved and unread counts: the app's Concerns tab badge), `GET /api/admin/concerns/{concern}` (marks it read for this admin), `POST …/replies {message, resolve}`, `PATCH …/resolve`, `PATCH …/reopen`. Tests: `Api/AdminConcernApiTest.php`.

## Not built yet
- No retention period for concerns or replies, and no email to the admin on a new concern (it shows in the bell).
- No live updates for the bell (it updates on the next page load), and no phone push for replies (the app shows them when it opens).

## Mobile app API (Sanctum; students, faculty and staff, 403 for the admin)
Same controllers, requests, limits and messages as the web wherever they exist:
- **Saved and recent places**: `GET /api/places` → `{saved, recent}` (`PlaceResource`: code, name, and `Room::locationLabel()` as `location`), newest first. `POST`/`DELETE /api/places/{room}/save`, `POST /api/places/{room}/recent` and `DELETE /api/places/recent` are the web's `PlaceController` (JSON when the request wants it; `throttle:60,1`). No rename: the web has none.
- **Concerns**: `POST /api/concerns` is the web's `ConcernController::store` (`StoreConcernRequest`, `throttle:5,10`; 201 with `message` and the concern). `GET /api/concerns` (`Api\ConcernController`): the newest `HelpController::RECENT_CONCERNS` with replies (`ConcernResource`, status open or resolved), `meta.email` and `meta.topics`; like opening the Help page, it marks the user's `ConcernReplied` notifications read.
- **Bell**: `GET /api/notifications` → `{unread_count, data}` (the newest `BELL_NOTIFICATIONS`: kind, concern_id, title, body, read, created_at), `POST /api/notifications/{id}/read` (own only, 404 otherwise), `POST /api/notifications/read` (all).
- App screens: `app/portal/saved-places.tsx` (plus the star on the navigation card, `toggleSavedPlace` in `lib/portal-data.ts`, and Recent on Start), `help.tsx`, `notifications.tsx` (the bell on Home). Saved places are kept on the phone for offline reading.
