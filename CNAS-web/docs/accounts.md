# Admin accounts and trusted alert senders

Read this before touching Admin > Accounts (list, create, delete) or who may send emergency alerts.

## Decisions
- **Admin > Accounts** (`/admin/accounts`, `Admin\AccountController`): everyone's account, newest first (no date last), 20 per page. The role bar at the top is also the role filter (each segment's width is that role's share); chips filter by role or "Can send alerts" (`?role=`, `?trusted=1`). Search (`?q=`) runs on the server through `User::scopeSearch`, so people's details never go to the browser in bulk. The admin top bar search opens a person's account page.
- **Create account** (`/admin/accounts/create`): faculty, staff or student, never admin. Faculty and staff can't register themselves (registration needs a student COR), so this is how their accounts get made; a student only when they can't register. Students need the 8-digit ID and a program; faculty may have a program; staff have none. The admin sets a temporary password (Generate, Show, Copy: `initPasswordTools` in `resources/js/accounts.js`) and gives it to the person, who must choose their own at the first sign-in (`must_change_password` is set on every account made here). It's never stored or shown again. `verified_at` stays empty (it means COR-verified). `email_verified_at` stays empty too: the person gets a code on their first sign-in and verifies their email before the portal opens (registration.md, "Email verification").
- **Delete** works like deleting a GitHub repository: the account page's Danger zone lists what goes (sign-ins, classes, saved places, "I'm safe" answers), and the admin types the account's email. The button is disabled until it matches (`accounts.js`); `DeleteAccountRequest` checks it again (case and spaces ignored). `AccountDeletion` also ends their web sessions and app tokens. Their push subscriptions become anonymous, so their phone keeps getting emergency alerts. Alerts they sent and concerns they wrote are kept without their name. Admin accounts, including your own, can't be deleted here.
- **Trusted for alerts**: `users.alert_trusted_at` (not fillable). Only faculty and staff can be trusted (`User::canBeTrustedForAlerts`); a role change away from those takes it away. `User::canSendAlerts()` = admin or trusted. The admin turns it on or off with the switch on the account page, or when creating the account.
- Trusted users get "Emergency alerts" in the faculty portal sidebar (`/faculty/alerts`, `Portal\AlertController`, middleware `EnsureUserCanSendAlerts`). They send an alert straight away (no approval) with the admin's own form (`partials/alert-send-form`, same `StoreAlertRequest`, `Alert::send`) and end the active one (`partials/alert-resolve-form`). Editing an alert's wording stays admin-only. Every alert shows who sent it, so the admin can see a trusted user sent it. Turning trust off takes effect on their next request.

- **Faculty or staff**: the account page switches someone between Faculty and Staff (`admin.accounts.role`), e.g. a staff member who also teaches. Faculty get "My classes"; staff don't (docs/schedule.md). Other roles can't be switched. Classes and alert trust stay as they are.

- **Edit details** (`/admin/accounts/{user}/edit`, `admin.accounts.update`, `Requests\Admin\UpdateAccountRequest`, `admin/account-edit-page`): from the account page's Details card. Name, ID, program and email, with the same rules as Create account for the account's role (students need the 8-digit ID and a program; staff have no program). Not the role (Faculty or staff switch above) and not the password (only its owner, in Settings). A changed email becomes unverified (`email_verified_at` null, pending codes forgotten): the person confirms it with a code at their next sign-in. Admin accounts aren't edited here (403); the admin uses Settings → My account. The account page shows "(not verified yet)" next to an unverified email.

- **Set temporary password** (account page, `admin.accounts.password`, `AccountController@password`, error bag `temporaryPassword`): for someone who forgot their password and can't reset it themselves (faculty and staff mostly; and anyone who can't open their email: check their school ID in person, fix the email with Edit details, then set this. Students who can open their email use "Forgot password?", registration.md "Password recovery"). Same Generate, Show, Copy tools (`form[data-password-tools]`). Sets the password and `users.must_change_password` (not fillable), rotates the remember token and ends all their sessions and app tokens. At the next sign-in they must choose their own ("Choose a new password", `EnsurePasswordIsChanged`). The account page shows "Waiting for them to change it" meanwhile. Not for admin accounts (403).

## Code shared with the mobile app
- The rules are in `App\Services\AccountAdministration` (create, `updateDetails`, `setTemporaryPassword`, `setTrusted`, `switchRole`, `canDelete`, `delete`) and the requests `StoreAccountRequest`, `UpdateAccountRequest`, `SetTemporaryPasswordRequest`, `SwitchRoleRequest`, `DeleteAccountRequest`. `Admin\AccountController` (web) and `Api\Admin\AccountController` (app) only call them. `AccountController::listQuery()`, `trusted()` and `programsByCollege()` are shared too.
- App API (admin only, `EnsureUserIsAdmin`): `GET /api/admin/accounts?q=&role=&trusted=1&page=` (10 a page, counts by role, trusted count), `GET /api/admin/accounts/options`, `GET/POST /api/admin/accounts`, `GET/PUT/DELETE /api/admin/accounts/{user}` (delete needs `confirm_email`), `PATCH …/trust {trusted}`, `…/password {password}`, `…/role {role}`. Every answer has `message`; the account answers have `can_delete`, `can_be_trusted`, `can_switch_role` and the deletion preview.
- Account changes aren't logged on the web, so the app doesn't log them either.
- App screens: the Accounts tab, `admin/account-view` (trust switch, Faculty or staff, temporary password with Generate, Danger zone with the email typed), `admin/account-form` (create or edit).
